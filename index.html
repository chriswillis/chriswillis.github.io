Chris Willis Chief Design Officer and Futurist, Domo

I'm a strategic design leader building products that feel genuinely human.

I'm the founding Chief Design Officer and Futurist at Domo. For 15 years I've helped build one of the best cloud-native, mobile-first data analytics platforms, and now with governed, agentic AI that real people can actually use. I take a first-principles approach to AI and innovation, starting with the human problem instead of the cool demo.
