Chris Willis Chief Design Officer and AI Strategy, Domo

I'm a design leader and product strategist helping put complex technology to work for people.

I'm the founding Chief Design Officer and Futurist at Domo. For 15 years, I've helped shape our cloud-native, mobile-first data analytics platform and, more recently, its approach to governed, agentic AI.

My focus is now on developing systems of context, knowledge, observability, and generative UI for agentic applications – understanding how agents work (or don't), who's using them, and what those people need to accomplish, then encoding the UX/UI judgement that's perfect for them.

Personalization is the new productivity. Enterprise software has long asked people to adapt to its way of working. I'm working toward software that adapts to each person's way of working.

Cue (alpha). Write your talk. Cue the slides. Behind every great presentation is a great story. Cue is a markdown editor I designed for writing presentations rather than wrestling with design tools. The layout automatically comes from what you write. It's fast, minimal and beautiful. For now, your slides are locally in your browser. Warning: It’s an early alpha, so beware and save often. Tool · Alpha Try We beat measles once but will it come back? Tracking US state cases. Measles vanished after 2000. Now it’s surging back as most states have slipped below the herd-immunity line. Data story Explore Some monsters are real. Tracking the 2026 El Niño in real-time Climatologists have warned a Godzilla El Niño was coming. As a way to better understand just how unique this situation is, we use a chart that warps the grid instead of the data, rebuilt from NOAA’s daily sea-surface temperatures every time it loads. The Niño 3.4 region temperatures are plotted against curves of standard deviations. Live data Explore A tribute to Luminet's first portrait of a black hole In 1978 nobody had seen a black hole, and nobody expected to. Jean-Pierre Luminet, a young astrophysicist at the Paris Observatory in Meudon, let the math show him something unique about the universe. He computed the view on an old IBM 7040 and painted the portrait by hand. I used his original equations to recreate his paper in the browser. Simulation Explore Yves Outlines. Generate posters with type that ripples Inspired by Yves Béhar's type treatment on Design Manifestos (from ICON magazine, Issue 50, August 2007). Tiny app grows thin concentric outlines around any letters and words, like rings spreading from a stone dropped in water. Choose a typeface, adjust the rings and the background color, and export your own manifesto as a png. Generator Try Palette DNA Map It started with Steph Ango's Flexoki, a palette inspired by ink on paper. Wrapping its colors around a 3D surface revealed a hidden shape, a kind of DNA, and the same method can look inside any palette. That surface then becomes the engine for a color picker. It shows where the palette has real colors and where it's filling in, and it can generate new colors shaped like the rest of the family. Color tool Try Palette DNA Ramp Turn a brand color into a ramp of light and dark steps, shaped by Tailwind, Radix, Flexoki or your own imported palette. Add a second color to guide the result, compare the ramp with its reference family, and check text contrast on light and dark backgrounds. Export CSS, Tailwind or design tokens, or share a link to your ramp. Color tool Try
